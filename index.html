Approach

I prefer shipping useful things over talking about perfect systems.

My work usually starts with a concrete problem: collecting leads, tracking operations, parsing messages, monitoring something physical, or building a tool that saves time.

I like systems that are small enough to move fast, but real enough to be useful. That means practical architecture, clear workflows, and quick iteration instead of endless planning.
